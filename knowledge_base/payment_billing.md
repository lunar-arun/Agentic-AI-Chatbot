# Payment & Billing — GreenTech Solutions

## Accepted Payment Methods

| Method | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Credit Cards** | Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover |
| **Debit Cards** | Any card with Visa/Mastercard logo |
| **PayPal** | PayPal account or PayPal Credit |
| **Store Credit** | GreenTech gift cards and store credit balances |
| **Apple Pay / Google Pay** | Available on mobile and supported browsers |

## When You Are Charged

- **Credit/Debit Cards:** Your card is authorized at checkout but charged only when your order ships.
- **PayPal:** Payment is captured immediately at checkout.
- **Store Credit/Gift Cards:** Deducted immediately at checkout.

## Sales Tax

Sales tax is calculated based on the shipping destination and applicable state and local tax laws. Tax amounts are displayed at checkout before you complete your purchase. GreenTech Solutions collects sales tax in all U.S. states where required by law.

## Invoices and Receipts

- A receipt is emailed to you automatically when your order is placed.
- A final invoice with tax details is sent when your order ships.
- You can download invoices from **My Account** → **Order History** → **Download Invoice**.

## Billing Issues

### Duplicate Charges
If you see a duplicate charge on your statement, it may be an authorization hold that will drop off within 3–5 business days. If the duplicate charge persists beyond 5 business days, contact us with your order number and a screenshot of your statement. We will investigate and issue a refund for the duplicate within 2 business days.

### Failed Payment
If your payment fails at checkout:
1. Verify your card number, expiration date, and CVV
2. Ensure your billing address matches what your bank has on file
3. Check that your card has sufficient funds or credit
4. Try a different payment method
5. Contact your bank if the issue persists — they may be blocking the transaction

### Price Adjustments
If an item you purchased goes on sale within 7 days of your purchase, contact us for a price adjustment. We will refund the difference to your original payment method. Price adjustments are limited to one per item and do not apply to clearance or Final Sale items.

### Promotional Codes
- Only one promotional code can be applied per order
- Promotional codes cannot be combined with other offers unless stated
- Codes are case-sensitive and must be entered exactly as provided
- Expired codes cannot be honored

## Subscriptions and Auto-Pay

Some GreenTech products (water filters, air filters) offer subscription auto-delivery:
- Subscriptions are billed on the renewal date shown in your account
- You can pause, skip, or cancel a subscription at any time from **My Account** → **Subscriptions**
- Changes must be made at least 48 hours before the next billing date
- Subscription orders receive a 15% discount automatically
