# Returns & Refunds Policy — GreenTech Solutions

## Our Guarantee

At GreenTech Solutions, we stand behind every product we sell. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we offer a straightforward return and refund process. We want you to feel confident shopping with us.

## Return Eligibility

### Eligible for Return
- Items in their original, unopened packaging within **30 days** of delivery
- Items that are defective, damaged, or materially different from what was described on our website, within **60 days** of delivery
- Items with a manufacturing defect discovered within the product's warranty period

### Not Eligible for Return
- Items that have been used, installed, or modified (unless defective)
- Gift cards and digital downloads
- Clearance items marked as "Final Sale"
- Items returned after the applicable return window has closed
- Items without a valid Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number

## How to Initiate a Return

1. **Request an RMA Number:** Log into your GreenTech Solutions account, go to "My Orders," select the order, and click "Request Return." Alternatively, email us at returns@greentechsolutions.example.com with your order number and reason for return.
2. **Receive Your RMA:** We will review your request and email you an RMA number and a prepaid return shipping label within 1–2 business days.
3. **Pack the Item:** Place the item in its original packaging (if available) and include the RMA number inside the box.
4. **Ship It Back:** Attach the prepaid shipping label and drop the package at any FedEx or USPS location.

## Refund Processing

- **Inspection:** Once we receive your return, our team will inspect the item within 3–5 business days.
- **Refund Issued:** If the return is approved, we will issue a refund to your original payment method. Refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank or credit card provider.
- **Partial Refunds:** If the item shows signs of use or is missing original accessories, we may issue a partial refund (up to 50% deduction).

## Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. To receive a different item, please return the original item for a refund and place a new order. If the replacement item costs more, you will be charged the difference. If it costs less, the balance will be refunded.

## Defective or Damaged Items

If you receive a defective or damaged item:
1. Contact us within **7 days** of delivery with photos of the damage.
2. We will send a replacement at no cost, or issue a full refund — your choice.
3. You do not need to return the damaged item in most cases. We may ask for photos for our quality records.

## Warranty Claims

Many of our products come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 1 to 5 years. To file a warranty claim:
1. Email warranty@greentechsolutions.example.com with your order number, product name, and a description of the issue.
2. Include photos or a video demonstrating the defect.
3. Our warranty team will respond within 3 business days with next steps.

Warranty coverage includes manufacturing defects and premature failure under normal use. It does not cover damage from misuse, accidents, unauthorized modifications, or normal wear and tear.

## Restocking Fees

- Standard returns (buyer's remorse, wrong size, etc.): **No restocking fee**
- Opened but unused items returned within 30 days: **10% restocking fee**
- Items returned without original packaging: **15% restocking fee**
- Freight/oversized items (solar panels, filtration systems): **20% restocking fee** to cover return shipping costs

## Return Shipping Costs

- **Defective/damaged/wrong items:** We cover all return shipping costs.
- **All other returns:** We provide a prepaid label; a $6.99 return shipping fee will be deducted from your refund.

## Late Returns

Returns received after the 30-day window but within 45 days may be accepted for store credit only (no cash refund). Returns received after 45 days will not be accepted.
