# Payment & Billing FAQ

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and store gift cards. Some regions also support buy-now-pay-later options at checkout, where available.

## Why was my payment declined?
Common reasons include:
- Insufficient funds or credit limit reached
- Incorrect card number, expiry date, or CVV entered
- Billing address doesn't match the address on file with your bank
- Your bank flagged the transaction as suspicious (common for first-time purchases or unusually large orders)
- The card has expired or been deactivated

If your payment is declined, try re-entering your details, using a different payment method, or contacting your bank to authorize the transaction before retrying.

## When will I be charged?
Your card is charged at the time the order is placed, not when it ships. For pre-orders or backordered items, you may be charged at checkout or at the time of shipment, depending on the product — this will be noted on the product page.

## Why do I see a pending charge that's different from my order total?
Some banks place a temporary authorization hold that may briefly show a different amount than your final order total. This hold is released automatically within 3-5 business days and is not an additional charge.

## How do refunds work?
Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. Processing times:
- Credit/debit card: 5-10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank
- PayPal: 3-5 business days
- Gift card: Refunded instantly as store credit

## Can I get an invoice or receipt for my order?
Yes. An order confirmation email doubling as a receipt is sent automatically at checkout. For a formal tax invoice (e.g. for business expensing), log into your account, go to "My Orders," and select "Download Invoice," or contact support to have one emailed to you.

## I was charged twice for the same order. What happened?
This is usually a temporary authorization hold appearing alongside the actual charge, not a duplicate charge — holds typically disappear within a few business days. If both charges are still showing as completed (not pending) after 5 business days, contact support with your order number so we can investigate and refund the duplicate if confirmed.

## Can I update my payment method for a subscription or recurring order?
Yes — go to "Account Settings" > "Payment Methods" to add, remove, or set a default card. Changes apply to your next billing cycle; they do not retroactively affect charges already processed.
