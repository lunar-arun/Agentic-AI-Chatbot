# Order Tracking — GreenTech Solutions

## How to Track Your Order

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email containing your tracking number and a direct link to the carrier's tracking page. You can also track your order by logging into your GreenTech Solutions account:

1. Go to **My Account** → **My Orders**
2. Click on the order you want to track
3. Click **Track Package** to view real-time status

## Tracking Statuses Explained

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Order Placed** | We have received your order and it is being processed |
| **Processing** | Your order is being picked and packed at our warehouse |
| **Shipped** | Your order has left our warehouse and is with the carrier |
| **In Transit** | Your package is moving through the carrier's network |
| **Out for Delivery** | Your package is on the delivery vehicle and will arrive today |
| **Delivered** | Your package has been delivered to the address on file |
| **Exception** | There is a delivery issue — see details on the tracking page |

## Estimated Delivery Windows

- **Standard Shipping:** 5–7 business days from ship date
- **Expedited Shipping:** 2–3 business days from ship date
- **Overnight Shipping:** Next business day (if ordered before 2 PM CT)
- **International (Canada):** 7–12 business days from ship date
- **International (UK/EU):** 10–18 business days from ship date

## Common Tracking Issues

### Tracking Not Updating
If your tracking information has not updated for more than 5 business days, the package may be delayed in transit. Contact us and we will investigate with the carrier. Please have your order number and tracking number ready.

### Package Shows Delivered But Not Received
1. Check around your property — porches, side doors, mailroom
2. Check with neighbors who may have accepted the package
3. Wait 24 hours — sometimes tracking updates before actual delivery
4. If still missing after 24 hours, contact us to file a lost package claim

### Wrong Tracking Number
If the tracking number in your email does not work or shows a different shipment, contact us immediately with your order number. We will provide the correct tracking information.
