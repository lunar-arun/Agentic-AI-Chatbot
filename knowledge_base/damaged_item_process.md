# Damaged Item Process — GreenTech Solutions

## If You Receive a Damaged Item

We take great care in packaging your orders, but occasionally items may be damaged during shipping. If you receive a damaged item, we will make it right — quickly and at no cost to you.

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Document the Damage
- Take clear photos of:
  - The damaged item (multiple angles)
  - The packaging and shipping box (showing any external damage)
  - The shipping label on the box
- Keep all original packaging materials — do not discard them

### Step 2: Contact Us Within 7 Days
Report the damage within **7 days of delivery** through any of these channels:
- **Email:** returns@greentechsolutions.example.com — attach your photos
- **Phone:** 1-800-555-GREEN (1-800-555-4733)
- **Online:** My Account → My Orders → select the order → Report Damage

Include the following information:
- Order number
- Item name and quantity damaged
- Description of the damage
- Photos (at least 2)

### Step 3: Choose Your Resolution
Once we confirm the damage, you can choose one of these options:

| Option | Details | Timeline |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Free Replacement** | We ship a new item at no cost | Ships within 1–2 business days |
| **Full Refund** | Refund to your original payment method | 5–10 business days |
| **Store Credit** | Credit applied to your GreenTech account (110% of item value as a goodwill bonus) | Immediate |

### Step 4: Return of Damaged Item
- **In most cases, you do NOT need to return the damaged item.** We may ask you to recycle it responsibly.
- For high-value items (over $200), we may provide a prepaid return label and ask you to send the item back.
- You will never be charged for return shipping on damaged items.

## Damage Discovered After Opening

If you discover concealed damage after opening the package (not visible from outside):
- You still have **7 days from delivery** to report it
- Follow the same process above
- Concealed damage is treated the same as visible damage

## Damage vs. Defect

| Type | Definition | Process |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Damage** | Item was broken, crushed, or harmed during shipping | Use this Damaged Item Process |
| **Defect** | Item has a manufacturing flaw (doesn't work properly, missing parts) | File a Warranty Claim (see Warranty section in Returns Policy) |

If you're unsure whether your item is damaged or defective, contact us and we'll help determine the right process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if the damage was caused by the carrier (FedEx/USPS)?**
A: You do not need to file a carrier claim yourself. We handle all carrier claims on your behalf. Just report the damage to us.

**Q: What if I discarded the packaging before noticing the damage?**
A: You can still report the damage, but please provide as many photos as possible. Having the original packaging strengthens the carrier claim, but it is not required for us to help you.

**Q: Can I get a replacement AND a refund?**
A: No, you must choose one resolution per damaged item. However, if the replacement also arrives damaged, we will issue a full refund immediately.

**Q: Is there a limit on how many damage claims I can file?**
A: We review damage claims on a case-by-case basis. Frequent claims may be flagged for additional review, but legitimate damage is always covered.
