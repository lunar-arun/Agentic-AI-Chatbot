# Order Tracking FAQ

## How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive an email and SMS with a tracking number and a link to the carrier's tracking page. You can also log into your account and go to "My Orders" to view live status at any time.

## What do the order statuses mean?
- **Processing** — Your order has been received and payment confirmed. It is being prepared for shipment. This typically takes 1-2 business days.
- **Packed** — Your order has been picked and packed at the warehouse and is awaiting carrier pickup.
- **Shipped** — Your order has left the warehouse and is with the carrier. Tracking updates should begin appearing within 24 hours.
- **Out for Delivery** — Your package is on the delivery vehicle and expected to arrive today.
- **Delivered** — The carrier has marked the package as delivered. If you haven't received it, see "What if my order shows delivered but I don't have it?" below.
- **Delayed** — An unexpected delay has occurred (weather, customs, carrier backlog). A new estimated delivery date will be shown.

## How long does delivery take?
- Standard shipping: 5-7 business days
- Expedited shipping: 2-3 business days
- Overnight shipping: 1 business day (order must be placed before 2 PM local time)
- International shipping: 7-21 business days depending on destination and customs processing

## My tracking number isn't updating. What should I do?
Tracking can take up to 24 hours to reflect the first scan after a label is created. If there's been no update for more than 3 business days after that, this usually means the package is delayed in transit — contact support with your order number so we can open a trace with the carrier.

## What if my order shows delivered but I don't have it?
1. Check around your property — porches, mailrooms, or with a neighbor, since carriers sometimes leave packages in an alternate safe location.
2. Wait 24 hours, as some carriers mark "delivered" up to a day before physical drop-off in rare cases.
3. If it still hasn't turned up, contact support — this is treated as a potential lost package and will be escalated for investigation and resolution (replacement or refund).

## Can I change my delivery address after the order has shipped?
Once an order has shipped, the delivery address generally cannot be changed, since the package is already with the carrier. Some carriers offer an "intercept" or redirect service for an additional fee — support can check if this is available for your specific shipment.

## Can I expedite an order that's already processing?
If your order hasn't shipped yet, contact support as soon as possible — we may be able to upgrade the shipping method for an additional charge, depending on how far along fulfillment is.
