# Cancellation Policy — GreenTech Solutions

## Order Cancellation Window

You may cancel an order **before it has been shipped**. Once an order enters the "Shipped" status, it cannot be canceled — you will need to follow our Returns process instead.

### How to Cancel an Order

1. **Online (fastest):** Log into your account → **My Orders** → select the order → click **Cancel Order**
2. **Email:** Send your order number to support@greentechsolutions.example.com with the subject line "Cancel Order"
3. **Phone:** Call 1-800-555-GREEN (1-800-555-4733) during business hours

### Cancellation Processing Times

| Method | Processing Time |
|--------|----------------|
| Online self-service | Immediate (if order is still in "Processing" status) |
| Email | Within 2 business hours |
| Phone | Immediate during business hours |

## Cancellation Eligibility by Order Status

| Order Status | Can Cancel? | Notes |
|-------------|-------------|-------|
| **Order Placed** | ✅ Yes | Full refund, no fees |
| **Processing** | ✅ Yes | Full refund, no fees |
| **Packed** | ⚠️ Maybe | Contact us immediately — we may be able to intercept |
| **Shipped** | ❌ No | Must use Returns process |
| **Delivered** | ❌ No | Must use Returns process |

## Refund After Cancellation

- Canceled orders are refunded to the original payment method
- **Credit/Debit Card refunds:** 3–5 business days
- **PayPal refunds:** 1–3 business days
- **Store Credit refunds:** Immediate
- No cancellation fees are charged

## Partial Cancellations

If your order contains multiple items, you can cancel individual items as long as they haven't shipped yet:
1. Go to **My Orders** → select the order
2. Click **Cancel Items** next to the specific item(s)
3. Remaining items in the order will still ship as scheduled

## Subscription Cancellations

To cancel a recurring subscription (auto-delivery):
1. Go to **My Account** → **Subscriptions**
2. Select the subscription you want to cancel
3. Click **Cancel Subscription**
4. Cancellation takes effect before the next billing cycle

**Important:** Cancellation must be made at least 48 hours before the next billing date. If a subscription order has already been processed, it cannot be canceled and must be returned.
