# Shipping Policy — GreenTech Solutions

## Overview

GreenTech Solutions ships all orders from our central warehouse located in Austin, Texas. We are committed to delivering your eco-friendly products quickly and sustainably. All shipments use carbon-neutral carriers whenever possible.

## Shipping Methods and Delivery Times

### Standard Shipping
- **Cost:** Free on orders over $50; $5.99 for orders under $50
- **Delivery Time:** 5–7 business days within the contiguous United States
- **Carrier:** USPS or FedEx Ground (carbon-offset program)

### Expedited Shipping
- **Cost:** $12.99 flat rate
- **Delivery Time:** 2–3 business days within the contiguous United States
- **Carrier:** FedEx Express

### Overnight Shipping
- **Cost:** $24.99 flat rate
- **Delivery Time:** Next business day if ordered before 2:00 PM CT
- **Carrier:** FedEx Priority Overnight

### Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. Territories
- **Cost:** $15.99 flat rate (standard) or $29.99 (expedited)
- **Delivery Time:** 7–14 business days (standard) or 3–5 business days (expedited)

## International Shipping

We currently ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union.

- **Canada:** $14.99 flat rate, 7–12 business days
- **United Kingdom:** $19.99 flat rate, 10–15 business days
- **European Union:** $22.99 flat rate, 10–18 business days

International customers are responsible for any customs duties, import taxes, or brokerage fees charged by their country. GreenTech Solutions is not responsible for delays caused by customs processing.

## Order Processing

- Orders placed before 2:00 PM CT on business days are processed the same day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM CT or on weekends/holidays are processed the next business day.
- You will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number once your order ships.
- During peak seasons (November–December), processing may take an additional 1–2 business days.

## Tracking Your Order

Once your order has shipped, you will receive an email with your tracking number and a link to the carrier's tracking page. You can also log into your GreenTech Solutions account and view your order status under "My Orders."

If your tracking information has not updated for more than 5 business days, please contact our support team at support@greentechsolutions.example.com or call 1-800-555-GREEN (1-800-555-4733).

## Shipping Restrictions

- We do not ship to P.O. Boxes for expedited or overnight orders.
- Hazardous materials (e.g., certain batteries, chemical cleaners) may have shipping restrictions and cannot be shipped via air.
- Some oversized items (solar panels, large water filtration systems) may require freight shipping. You will be contacted with a freight quote before your order is processed.

## Lost or Damaged Shipments

If your package is lost or arrives damaged:
1. Contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date.
2. Provide your order number, tracking number, and photos of any damage.
3. We will file a claim with the carrier and either reship the item at no cost or issue a full refund within 5–10 business days.

## Address Changes

If you need to change your shipping address after placing an order, contact us immediately. We can update the address only if the order has not yet been shipped. Once an order is in transit, we cannot redirect the package.
