# Account Help — GreenTech Solutions

## Creating an Account

1. Visit greentechsolutions.example.com and click **Sign Up**
2. Enter your email address and create a password (minimum 8 characters, must include a number and a special character)
3. Fill in your name and shipping address
4. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox
5. Your account is ready to use

## Logging In

- Go to greentechsolutions.example.com and click **Log In**
- Enter your registered email and password
- If you have two-factor authentication enabled, enter the code sent to your phone

## Password Reset

If you forgot your password:
1. Click **Forgot Password?** on the login page
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for a password reset link
4. Click the link and create a new password
5. The reset link expires after 1 hour

If you do not receive the reset email within 5 minutes:
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify you are using the correct email address
- Contact support for manual reset assistance

## Updating Account Information

You can update the following from **My Account** → **Account Settings**:
- **Name:** Edit your first and last name
- **Email:** Change your email address (requires re-verification)
- **Password:** Change your password (requires current password)
- **Phone Number:** Add or update your phone number
- **Shipping Addresses:** Add, edit, or remove saved addresses
- **Payment Methods:** Add or remove saved cards (we do not store full card numbers)
- **Communication Preferences:** Opt in or out of marketing emails, SMS notifications, and order updates

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

For added security, you can enable 2FA:
1. Go to **My Account** → **Security Settings**
2. Click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**
3. Choose SMS or authenticator app
4. Follow the setup instructions
5. Save your backup recovery codes in a safe place

## Account Deletion

To delete your GreenTech Solutions account:
1. Go to **My Account** → **Account Settings** → **Delete Account**
2. Confirm your password
3. Select a reason for leaving (optional)
4. Click **Permanently Delete My Account**

**Important notes about account deletion:**
- Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
- All saved addresses, payment methods, and preferences will be removed
- Order history will be retained for 90 days for legal and tax purposes, then permanently deleted
- Any active subscriptions will be canceled
- Store credit and gift card balances will be forfeited
- You will receive a confirmation email after deletion is processed (within 24 hours)

## Data Privacy

- GreenTech Solutions does not sell your personal information
- You can request a copy of all data we have about you from **Account Settings** → **Download My Data**
- Data export requests are processed within 30 days
- For more information, see our Privacy Policy at greentechsolutions.example.com/privacy
